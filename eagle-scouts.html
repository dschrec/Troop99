Eagle Scout Program

The highest rank in Scouting — and the beginning of a lifetime of leadership

The Highest Honor

Path to Eagle Scout

Troop 99 has a proud tradition of producing Eagle Scouts. Through merit badges, leadership roles, and a service project that makes a real difference, our youth achieve the highest rank in Scouting.

The Journey

Steps to Eagle

1

Join Troop 99

Boys and girls ages 10-17 are welcome. Come to any meeting to see what we do.

2

Earn Merit Badges

Complete 13+ merit badges in areas like science, technology, outdoor skills, and more.

3

Advance Through Ranks

Progress through Tenderfoot, Second Class, First Class, Star, and Life ranks.

4

Leadership Position

Serve in a leadership role within the troop — patrol leader, senior patrol leader, or more.

5

Eagle Project

Plan, fundraise for, and lead a significant service project that benefits your community.

6

Achieve Eagle Scout

Complete your application and board of review. Welcome to the Eagle Scouts!
