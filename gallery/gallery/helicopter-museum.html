Helicopter Museum Overnight

February 2026 — An overnight among helicopters

Troop 99 scouts spent an overnight at the Helicopter Museum, exploring real aircraft up close — from cockpits and rotors to a hands-on presentation in the hangar. It was a rare night of aviation adventure right in our backyard.
