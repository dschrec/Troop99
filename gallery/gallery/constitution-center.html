Constitution Center Scout Day

January 2025 — Exploring the history of our nation's founding

Our scouts visited the National Constitution Center in Philadelphia to learn about American democracy and the founding of our nation. The group toured the exhibits, participated in a mock trial, and discussed the Bill of Rights. It was a meaningful day that connected our scouts with American history.
