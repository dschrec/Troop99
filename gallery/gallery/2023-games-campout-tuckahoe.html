Games Campout 2023 — The Castle

Games, food, and fun at the Medieval Castle, Camp Tuckahoe

Troop 99’s 2023 Winter Games Campout brought the troop to the Medieval Castle at Camp Tuckahoe Scout Reservation — walking the castle walls, trading games, and sharing meals in the great hall. This year (2026) we’re headed right back to the same castle for the annual Games Campout!
