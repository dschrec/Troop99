Memorial Day Parade 2026

Honoring our fallen heroes in Newtown

Troop 99 scouts marched proudly in the Newtown Memorial Day Parade, honoring those who served our country. Our scouts carried flags, marched in formation, and represented our troop with pride and discipline. This tradition continues to teach our scouts about service and sacrifice.
