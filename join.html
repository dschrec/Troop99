Join Troop 99

Adventure, friendship, and leadership start here

Who Can Join?

Boys and girls ages 10-17 are welcome! No prior scouting experience needed. Come to any meeting and see what we do.

When & Where

Monday evenings at 7:00 PM
American Legion Post 440
Newtown, PA 18940

What to Bring

Just yourself! A sense of adventure, willingness to try new things, and a good attitude. We'll help the rest.

Cost

$150 Washington Crossing Council Registration
$190 Troop 99 Dues
Total: $340/year
(Venmo available with fees)

Getting Started

Three Simple Steps

1

Attend a Meeting

Come to any Monday evening meeting. No obligation — just come see what we do.

2
