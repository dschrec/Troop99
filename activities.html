Our Activities

Real adventures that build leaders — from summer camp to Eagle projects

OSR Summer Camp

Two unforgettable weeks at Ockanickon Council Reservation — swimming, canoeing, hiking, campfires, s'mores, and the Troop Campout. Our highlight event where we earn ranks, forge friendships, and create memories that last a lifetime.

View Photos →

Whitewater Rafting

Navigate the Lehigh River and other waterways with professional guides. Rafting trips combine thrilling whitewater action with beautiful river scenery and overnight camping. A true team-building challenge that builds confidence and trust.

View Photos →

Hiking & Backpacking

From Valley Forge to West Point, exploring historic sites and beautiful trails. Multi-day backpacking trips teach navigation, wilderness skills, and self-reliance. We've hiked the Appalachian Trail, climbed mountains, and explored state parks across the region.

View Photos →

Cycling & Canoeing

Two-wheeled adventures and river trips! We cycle through Bucks County and beyond, learning bike maintenance, laws & safety, and riding techniques. Canoe trips on Neshaminy Creek and the Delaware River combine paddling skill with riverside camping.

View Photos →

Eagle Scout Projects

Troop 99 Scouts lead community improvement projects that make a lasting difference. From building benches and free libraries to organizing food drives and conservation projects, Eagle candidates plan, lead, and execute service that benefits our community for years.

Learn More →

Camporees

Weekend camping competitions with other troops — West Point Camporee, Six Flags Camporee, and more. Camporees challenge Scouts with outdoor skills, teamwork, and friendly competition. We compete in knot tying, first aid, cooking, and more while having fun.

View Photos →

Community Service

From the Memorial Day Parade to MLK Service Projects, Troop 99 gives back year-round. Conservation work at Tyler Park, cleaning trails, helping at FEASTival, and supporting local organizations. Scouts learn that service is at the heart of Scouting.

View Photos →

Court of Honor & Pack Crossover

Celebrating achievements with family and community! Court of Honor ceremonies recognize Scouts who've earned rank advancements, merit badges, and special awards. Pack Crossover welcomes new Cub Scouts into the Troop, beginning their adventure with us.

View Photos →

Troop Camping

Regular camping trips throughout the year — from hidden valleys to Lehigh Valley Gorge. Monthly campouts build camping skills, leadership, and friendships. Scouts learn cooking, tent pitching, leave no trace, and outdoor safety on every trip.

View Photos →
