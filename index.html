About Troop 99

Welcoming Young Leaders
to the Outdoors

Troop 99 is a family troop serving Newtown, Pennsylvania, welcoming both boys and girls ages 10–17 to participate in the Scouting experience. We meet every Monday evening at 7:00 PM at the American Legion Post 440 hall.

Through camping, hiking, whitewater rafting, community service, and work toward the rank of Eagle Scout, our youth develop confidence, leadership skills, and friendships that last a lifetime.
